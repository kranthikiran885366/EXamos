# E1 false-positive and fairness status

**Status:** Not evaluated; no approved labelled dataset or qualified sensor hardware is available.

Potential ambiguity includes shared housing and adjacent rooms, dense access-point environments, legitimate accessibility/audio peripherals, randomized identifiers, radio attenuation and multipath, Wi-Fi roaming, sensor outages, camera obstruction, lighting, and coincidental time overlap. A visible radio signal does not establish device ownership, candidate proximity, or intent.

Before any evaluation claim, define the exact observation task and ground truth, lawfully source and minimize data, record hardware/driver/configuration and annotation method, separate development and evaluation data, and report denominators and uncertainty. Do not report precision, recall, false-positive rates, reviewer agreement, or demographic fairness without suitable governed labels and a reproducible method.

No candidate-level score or automated decision may be based solely on E1 environmental signals. Human review and an appeal path are required.
