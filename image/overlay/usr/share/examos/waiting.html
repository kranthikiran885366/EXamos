Welcome to ExamOS

An enterprise-grade, zero-trust examination operating system engineered for fair, secure, and resilient high-stakes assessments.

Total System Lockdown

All unauthorized processes, external storage devices, key combinations, and secondary displays are strictly quarantined at the Linux kernel level.

Zero Data Loss Protocol

Every response and keystroke is encrypted in real time to local memory and non-volatile buffers. If network connectivity drops, you continue testing without interruption.

Cryptographic Integrity

Exam sessions are digitally sealed using HMAC-SHA256 chained audit ledgers and hardware-backed TPM 2.0 attestation, ensuring verifiable authenticity.

Academic Integrity & Security Protocol

Please read the mandatory conduct rules carefully. All candidates are held to strict compliance.

Strictly Prohibited Items

  • Mobile phones, smartwatches, and wireless earbuds.
  • Unauthorized notes, physical textbooks, or formula sheets.
  • Secondary monitors, external keyboards, or mice with macro memory.
  • Speaking to others or looking away from the screen repeatedly.

Permitted Actions & Tools

  • Drinking water in a clear, unlabelled transparent container.
  • Using the embedded scientific calculator provided in the exam UI.
  • Utilizing the digital scratchpad built into the exam toolbar.
  • Raising a digital hand to contact the proctor via silent text chat.
1
Automated Incident Detection: The workstation monitors audio, gaze direction, and presence. Sudden departures from the camera frame or multiple faces detected will trigger an academic review flag.
2
Lockdown Isolation: Operating system shortcuts (Alt+Tab, Super key, Ctrl+Alt+Del, print screen) are neutralized. Attempting physical hardware manipulation immediately invalidates the attestation ticket.

How to Navigate Your Examination

A quick reference guide on question types, saving answers, review flags, and final submission.

1. Question Palette & Colors

The sidebar shows your progress using clear status color codes:

■ Green: Answered & encrypted
■ Amber: Flagged for review
■ Red: Visited but unanswered

2. Automatic Keystroke Encryption

You do not need to click "Save" after every question. Every option selected and text character typed is saved locally within milliseconds. A green checkmark confirms write integrity.

Access the scratchpad from the header. Notes are saved to your session and can be recalled at any time. Rich text questions support math equations and syntax-highlighted code blocks.

Before your final submission, you will see a comprehensive review table. You must confirm that all required sections are completed before the cryptographically signed receipt is generated.
